# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility and apologizing when mistakes happen
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, abusive language, or discriminatory behavior
- Trolling, insults, or personal/political attacks
- Public or private intimidation
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior and may take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces
when an individual is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through a private
security/contact channel before opening a public issue.

Maintainers will review and investigate complaints promptly and fairly.
